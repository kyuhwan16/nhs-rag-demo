Title: When and How to Use NHS 111

NHS 111 is a free, 24-hour telephone and online service for urgent but non-life-threatening medical concerns. It can be reached by calling 111 or using the NHS 111 online triage tool.

Typical situations for using NHS 111 include: a sudden worsening illness, uncertainty about whether to go to A&E, needing advice on medication, or requiring an out-of-hours GP appointment when the local surgery is closed.

NHS 111 is not for emergencies. Life-threatening situations such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, or loss of consciousness require calling 999 or going directly to A&E (Accident and Emergency).

International students who are unfamiliar with the NHS structure often default to A&E for problems that could be resolved faster and more appropriately through NHS 111 or a routine GP appointment, which can lead to long A&E waiting times and delayed care for genuine emergencies.
