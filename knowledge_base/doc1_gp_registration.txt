Title: How to Register with a GP in the UK

International students can register with a GP (General Practitioner) surgery near their home address, regardless of immigration status or how long they plan to stay. Registration is usually free through the NHS.

To register, find a local GP surgery using the NHS website "Find a GP" service, then complete a GMS1 registration form, either online or on paper at the surgery. Most surgeries ask for proof of address (a tenancy agreement, utility bill, or university accommodation letter) and photo ID (passport or BRP), although by law a GP practice cannot refuse to register someone simply because they lack these documents.

After registering, it usually takes a few days to a couple of weeks for the registration to be confirmed and for the patient to receive an NHS number. Some surgeries offer immediate access for urgent problems even before registration is fully processed.

Students should register with a GP as soon as they arrive in the UK, rather than waiting until they feel unwell, since same-day appointments for non-registered patients are limited.
